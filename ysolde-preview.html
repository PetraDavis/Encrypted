Ysolde

The Static Woman

.... . .-.. .--.

Twelve thousand years ago Ysolde was a scientist of the Veyra. Jealous co-workers sealed her inside her own laboratory and left her there.

She built a transmitter to send a distress signal. It worked too well. The signal took her in, and she became part of it.

Now she lives in electronics. Lights die as she comes near, and she speaks only in the rhythm of Morse code. She does not know she is a monster. She still thinks she is calling for help.

If she catches you, she pulls you into the signal with her.

Origin
Veyra laboratory, c. 12,000 years ago
Habitat
Screens, radios, wiring
Height
About 2.4 m
Tell
